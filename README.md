# Lux Mercatus - Online Grocery Shopping





Luxs Mercatus



Luxs Mercatus is a PHP-based online grocery shopping and e-commerce platform designed to provide customers with a convenient way to browse products, manage their shopping cart, place orders, and complete payments online.



The platform supports two main user roles: Customers and Seller/Admin.



> Features



> Shopping



\- Home page with featured products, deals, categories, and recommendations

\- Product listing and category pages

\- Product search

\- Product details with images, descriptions, prices, reviews, and variants

\- Deals and special offers

\- Category landing pages



> Customer



\- Customer registration and login

\- Profile management

\- Wishlist

\- Shopping cart

\- Address management

\- Payment methods

\- Order history

\- Order details

\- Order status tracking



Checkout \& Payment



* Cart management
* Checkout process
* Delivery/shipping address
* QR-code-based payment
* Order confirmation
* Order tracking



> Seller / Admin



* Secure admin login
* Product management
* Category management
* Inventory management
* Order management
* Customer management
* Offers and discount management
* Payment/order verification



> Technology



\- PHP

\- MySQL

\- HTML5

\- CSS3

\- JavaScript

\- Bootstrap -(if used in the implementation)-



> Project Status



Luxs Mercatus is being developed as a GitHub-based e-commerce project. The initial version is designed for local development using PHP and MySQL, with the possibility of deployment to a live web server in the future.



Product images, banners, and other visual assets can be added or replaced as the project develops.



> Objective



The goal of Luxs Mercatus is to create a simple, modern, and user-friendly online shopping platform that connects customers with products while providing administrators with the tools required to manage the store efficiently.
